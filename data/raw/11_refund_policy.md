# Refund Policy

Document ID: FP-PAY-011 | Owner: Payments Operations | Version 1.8 | Effective: 1 January 2026

## Merchant refunds to FinPay cards
When a merchant issues a refund to a FinPay card, the refund normally appears in the customer's wallet within **3 to 5 business days**. Some international merchants may take up to 10 business days. FinPay cannot speed up a refund that the merchant has not yet sent.

## Pending vs. completed
A pending card transaction that is cancelled by the merchant usually disappears within 7 days. If it does not, the customer can contact support.

## Refunds of FinPay fees
- Fees charged in error are refunded in full within 2 business days of confirmation.
- Monthly Plus or Business fees are not refunded for a partial month, except where FinPay made an error.

## Failed transfers
If an outgoing transfer fails or is rejected by the receiving bank, the amount is returned to the customer's wallet. Domestic transfers are returned within 1 business day; international transfers within 5 business days. The international transfer fee is refunded if the failure was not caused by incorrect details provided by the customer.

## Merchants issuing refunds
Merchants can refund a transaction in full or in part from the Merchant Dashboard or the API within **180 days** of the original payment. After 180 days, a refund must be sent as a separate payout.
