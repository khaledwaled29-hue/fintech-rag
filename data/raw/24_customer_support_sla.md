# Customer Support Service Levels

Document ID: FP-SUP-024 | Owner: Customer Support | Version 3.1 | Effective: 1 February 2026

## Channels and hours
| Channel | Availability | First response target |
|---|---|---|
| In-app chat | 24/7 | 5 minutes |
| Email | 24/7 (handled 08:00-20:00 local time) | 24 hours |
| Phone (general) | 08:00-20:00, Monday to Saturday | 3 minutes average wait |
| Card hotline (lost/stolen) | 24/7 | Immediate |

## Merchant support priority levels
| Priority | Example | Response target |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Merchant cannot accept any payments | 1 hour, 24/7 |
| P2 | Settlement delayed or partial outage | 4 business hours |
| P3 | General question or report request | 1 business day |

## Identity verification on contact
Before discussing account details, agents verify the customer through the in-app chat (already authenticated) or by sending a verification push to the customer's device for phone calls. Agents never ask for passwords, PINs or one-time passcodes.

## Languages
Support is available in English and Arabic.

## Escalation
If a customer is not satisfied with the answer, the agent offers to raise a formal complaint (see FP-SUP-025).
