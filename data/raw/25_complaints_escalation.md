# Complaints Handling and Escalation

Document ID: FP-SUP-025 | Owner: Customer Support | Version 2.0 | Effective: 1 February 2026

## What counts as a complaint
Any expression of dissatisfaction, spoken or written, about FinPay's products or service, where the customer expects a response.

## How to complain
In the app (Help > Make a complaint), by email to the complaints team, or by telling any support agent. Agents must log every complaint in the complaints system.

## Timeline
1. **Acknowledgement** within **2 business days**, with a complaint reference number.
2. **Final response** within **15 business days**. In exceptional cases FinPay may send a holding response explaining the delay, and the final response must then be sent within 35 business days.

## External escalation
If the customer is unhappy with the final response, or has not received one within 35 business days, they can refer the complaint to the Financial Services Ombudsman free of charge. The final response letter must include the Ombudsman's contact details.

## Redress
Where FinPay made a mistake, it will correct it, refund any fees and lost interest, and may offer compensation for distress or inconvenience.

## Root cause analysis
Complaint trends are reviewed monthly by the Customer Support and Product teams to fix underlying issues.
