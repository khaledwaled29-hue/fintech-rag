# Card Issuance and Activation

Document ID: FP-CRD-007 | Owner: Cards Operations | Version 2.2 | Effective: 1 January 2026

## Virtual cards
Every verified customer receives a virtual card instantly in the app. It can be added to mobile wallets and used online immediately.

## Physical cards
- Available to Plus and Business customers.
- Ordered in the app; delivery takes **5 to 7 business days**.
- If the card has not arrived after 10 business days, the customer should contact support, and the card will be cancelled and reissued free of charge.

## Activation
1. Open the FinPay app and go to Cards.
2. Select "Activate physical card".
3. Enter the last 4 digits printed on the card.
4. Set a 4-digit PIN in the app.
The card works for chip and PIN and contactless payments immediately after activation. The first contactless payment may require PIN entry.

## Card details
Cards are valid for 4 years. A replacement card is sent automatically 30 days before expiry to the address on file.

## PIN
The PIN can be viewed or changed at any time in the app after biometric or passcode authentication. Three wrong PIN entries block the PIN; it can be unblocked in the app.
