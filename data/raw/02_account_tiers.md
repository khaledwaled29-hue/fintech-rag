# Account Tiers

Document ID: FP-ACC-002 | Owner: Product | Version 2.4 | Effective: 1 January 2026

FinPay offers three account tiers for customers. A customer can upgrade at any time from the app by completing the extra verification for the higher tier.

## Basic
- Verification: government-issued photo ID and a selfie liveness check.
- Monthly fee: free.
- Includes: FinPay Wallet, one virtual card, domestic transfers.
- International transfers are **not** available on Basic.

## Plus
- Verification: everything required for Basic, plus a proof of address dated within the last 3 months (utility bill, bank statement or tenancy agreement).
- Monthly fee: USD 4.99.
- Includes: physical card, international transfers, 1% cashback on card purchases (see Rewards Program, FP-PRD-030), and two fee-free ATM withdrawals per month.

## Business
- Verification: Know Your Business (KYB) checks described in FP-KYC-004.
- Monthly fee: USD 19.00.
- Includes: up to 10 team cards, multi-user access with roles, bulk payouts, and API access.

## Downgrades
A customer may downgrade at the end of a billing month. Features of the higher tier stop on the downgrade date. Cashback already earned is still paid.

## Limits
Each tier has its own transaction limits. See Transaction Limits (FP-ACC-005).
