# FinPay Company Overview

Document ID: FP-GEN-001 | Owner: Corporate Communications | Version 3.2 | Effective: 1 March 2026

## Who we are
FinPay is a digital payments company founded in 2019. FinPay provides mobile wallets, debit cards, domestic and international money transfers, and merchant payment acceptance (card acquiring) for small and medium-sized businesses. FinPay serves approximately 1.2 million personal customers and 18,000 merchants.

## Products
- **FinPay Wallet**: a mobile app account that holds a balance in USD and supports instant transfers to other FinPay users.
- **FinPay Card**: a debit card linked to the wallet, available as a virtual card (instant) and a physical card.
- **FinPay Global Transfer**: international money transfers to 40 supported countries.
- **FinPay Merchant**: online and in-store card acceptance, with next-day settlement for established merchants.
- **FinPay API**: a developer platform that lets partners and merchants integrate payments, payouts and webhooks.

## Account tiers
Personal customers hold one of three tiers: Basic, Plus or Business. Tiers differ in verification requirements, monthly fees and transaction limits. See the Account Tiers document (FP-ACC-002) for details.

## Mission and values
FinPay's mission is to make everyday payments fast, transparent and safe. Our values are: customer first, security by design, clear pricing with no hidden fees, and compliance with all applicable financial regulations.

## Key departments
Customer Support, Risk & Fraud, Compliance (KYC/AML), Merchant Operations, Engineering, and Finance & Settlement. Internal policies in this knowledge base are owned by these departments.
