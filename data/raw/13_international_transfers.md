# International Transfers

Document ID: FP-PAY-013 | Owner: Payments Operations | Version 2.6 | Effective: 1 April 2026

## Availability
International transfers are available to Plus and Business customers only. FinPay supports transfers to 40 countries; the full list is shown in the app when choosing a recipient.

## Fees
A flat fee of USD 5.00 per transfer, plus the FX markup described in FP-FIN-014. The total cost and the exact amount the recipient receives are shown before the customer confirms.

## Delivery times
- Transfers sent through the SWIFT network: **1 to 3 business days**.
- Transfers to supported local payout partners: usually the same day.
- The cut-off for same-day processing is **14:00 UTC** on business days.

## Limits
Maximum single transfer: USD 25,000 (Plus) and USD 100,000 (Business). Daily and monthly tier limits also apply (FP-ACC-005).

## Required recipient details
Full name as shown on the bank account, bank name, IBAN or account number, SWIFT/BIC code, and recipient address for some countries.

## Cancellation
A transfer can be cancelled free of charge in the app while its status is "Processing". Once the status is "Sent", it can only be recalled through the receiving bank, which may take several weeks and is not guaranteed.

## Compliance checks
Some transfers are held for a compliance review, which normally takes up to 1 business day. The customer may be asked for the purpose of the payment or supporting documents.
