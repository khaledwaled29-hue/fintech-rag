# Dormant Accounts

Document ID: FP-ACC-018 | Owner: Customer Operations | Version 1.4 | Effective: 1 January 2026

## Definition
An account becomes **dormant** after **12 months** with no customer-initiated activity. Customer-initiated activity includes logging in to the app, making a payment or transfer, or using the card. Incoming payments, interest or cashback credits do not count.

## Notifications
FinPay emails and sends an app notification **30 days before** an account becomes dormant.

## Effect of dormancy
- The card is suspended and the account is read-only.
- No inactivity fees are charged.
- The balance remains safe and belongs to the customer.

## Reactivation
To reactivate, the customer logs in and completes a short re-verification: a new selfie liveness check, and an updated ID if the previous one has expired. Reactivation is usually completed within 1 business day.

## Long-term dormancy
After 6 years of dormancy, balances are handled according to local unclaimed-funds rules, and the customer can still reclaim them by contacting support.
