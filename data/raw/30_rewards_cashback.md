# Rewards and Cashback Program

Document ID: FP-PRD-030 | Owner: Product | Version 1.5 | Effective: 1 January 2026

## Who is eligible
Plus customers earn cashback. Basic and Business accounts do not earn cashback.

## How much
- **1% cashback** on eligible card purchases.
- Cashback is capped at **USD 20 per calendar month**.

## Excluded transactions
Cashback is not earned on: ATM withdrawals, transfers, wallet top-ups, gambling, purchases of cryptocurrency, money orders, or payments that are later refunded or charged back.

## When cashback is paid
Cashback earned in a month is credited to the customer's wallet by the **5th day of the following month**. It appears as "FinPay Cashback" in the transaction list.

## Refunds and disputes
If a purchase that earned cashback is refunded or successfully charged back, the related cashback is deducted from the next cashback payment.

## Program changes
FinPay may change the cashback rate or cap with at least 30 days' notice to customers.
