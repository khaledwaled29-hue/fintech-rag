# KYC Onboarding for Individual Customers

Document ID: FP-KYC-003 | Owner: Compliance | Version 4.1 | Effective: 15 February 2026

## Purpose
Know Your Customer (KYC) checks confirm a customer's identity before they can use FinPay services, in line with anti-money-laundering obligations.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be at least 18 years old and resident in a supported country.

## Required information
1. Full legal name, date of birth, nationality and residential address.
2. A valid government-issued photo ID: passport, national ID card, or driving licence. Expired documents are rejected.
3. A selfie liveness check taken in the FinPay app, compared automatically with the ID photo.
4. For the Plus tier: a proof of address dated within the last 3 months.

## Verification timeline
- Automated verification completes within 24 hours for most applicants.
- If an application is sent to manual review, the Compliance team completes it within 3 business days.
- Customers are notified by push notification and email of the outcome.

## Common rejection reasons
Blurry or cropped ID images, name mismatch between ID and application, selfie failing the liveness check, and proof of address older than 3 months. Customers may resubmit up to 3 times; after the third failure, the application is escalated to manual review.

## Periodic re-verification
Standard-risk customers are re-verified every 2 years. High-risk customers are re-verified every 12 months. Customers who do not complete re-verification within 30 days of the request have outgoing transactions restricted until they do.
