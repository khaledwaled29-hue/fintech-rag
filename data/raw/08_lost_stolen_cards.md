# Lost or Stolen Cards

Document ID: FP-CRD-008 | Owner: Cards Operations | Version 3.0 | Effective: 1 January 2026

## Immediate action
Customers should freeze the card instantly in the app (Cards > Freeze). Freezing is reversible, so it is the right first step if the card might just be misplaced.

If the card is confirmed lost or stolen, the customer should report it in the app (Cards > Report lost/stolen) or by calling the 24/7 card hotline. Reporting permanently cancels the card.

## Replacement
A new card is ordered automatically when a card is reported lost or stolen. Delivery takes 5 to 7 business days. A new virtual card is available instantly, so the customer can keep paying online.

Replacement fee: USD 10.00, except that the first replacement in any 12-month period is free when the card was reported stolen.

## Liability for unauthorized transactions
- After the card is reported, the customer is **not liable** for any unauthorized transactions.
- For unauthorized transactions before reporting, liability is limited to USD 50 if the customer reports within 2 business days of discovering the loss.
- Customers who act fraudulently or with gross negligence (for example, writing the PIN on the card) may be fully liable.

## Disputing transactions
Unauthorized transactions should be disputed through the chargeback process (FP-DSP-009).
