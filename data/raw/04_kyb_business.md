# KYB Onboarding for Business Accounts

Document ID: FP-KYC-004 | Owner: Compliance | Version 2.0 | Effective: 15 February 2026

## Purpose
Know Your Business (KYB) checks verify that a business is legally registered and identify the people who own and control it.

## Required documents
1. Certificate of incorporation or business registration.
2. Articles of association or equivalent constitutional document.
3. Proof of business address dated within the last 3 months.
4. Government ID for every director.
5. Identification of all Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBOs). A UBO is any individual who owns or controls **25% or more** of the business. Each UBO completes individual KYC (FP-KYC-003).
6. A short description of the business activity and expected monthly transaction volume.

## Review timeline
KYB reviews are completed within 5 business days once all documents are received. Incomplete applications are paused, and the applicant is notified of missing documents.

## Prohibited businesses
FinPay does not open Business accounts for unlicensed gambling operators, unlicensed cryptocurrency exchanges, weapons dealers, or shell companies with no verifiable activity. See also Merchant Onboarding (FP-MER-029).

## Ongoing monitoring
Business accounts are reviewed annually. Changes in ownership or directors must be reported to FinPay within 30 days.
