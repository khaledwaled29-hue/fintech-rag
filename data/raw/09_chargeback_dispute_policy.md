# Chargeback and Card Dispute Policy (Customers)

Document ID: FP-DSP-009 | Owner: Disputes Team | Version 4.0 | Effective: 1 March 2026

## What is a chargeback?
A chargeback is a request to reverse a card transaction through the card network when a customer has a valid dispute with a merchant or did not authorize the payment.

## Valid reasons
- Unauthorized transaction (card lost, stolen or details compromised).
- Goods or services not received.
- Goods significantly not as described or defective.
- Duplicate charge or incorrect amount.
- Cancelled subscription that was still charged.

## Time limits
- Customers must raise a dispute within **60 calendar days** of the transaction date.
- For goods or services not received, the 60 days start from the expected delivery date, up to a maximum of 120 days from the transaction date.

## Before raising a dispute
For non-fraud disputes (for example, not as described), the customer should first contact the merchant and allow the merchant 15 days to respond, unless the merchant is unreachable.

## How to raise a dispute
In the app: select the transaction > "Report a problem" > choose the reason > upload evidence (receipts, emails, photos).

## Provisional credit
For eligible disputes, FinPay gives a provisional credit within **10 business days** while the case is investigated.

## Resolution
Most disputes are resolved within **45 days**. Complex cases involving the card network's arbitration may take up to 90 days. If the dispute is decided in the merchant's favour, the provisional credit is reversed and the customer is notified at least 5 days before the reversal.
